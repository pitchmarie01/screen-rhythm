Why Screen Rhythm uses your camera

To notice your blinking rhythm during a screen session, Screen Rhythm uses Presage SmartSpectra face analysis on your webcam video.

  • The camera turns on only when you start a session, and off when you stop.
  • Video is processed live on this computer. It is never recorded, saved, or uploaded.
  • No account and no cloud storage.
  • This is a wellness companion, not a medical device. It doesn't diagnose anything.
